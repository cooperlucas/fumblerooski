Sports scores with no betting odds

Just the game. No spreads next to the score, no odds on the box score, no "bet now" buttons, no sportsbook promos between games.

Open Fumblerooski

Free. No account. Works on iPhone and Android: open it, then Add to Home Screen.

Why no odds?

Open most sports apps now and the betting line sits right next to the score, a sportsbook ad loads before the box score, and the "game preview" is really a parlay pitch. Fumblerooski was built by a football coach and fan who just wanted to know if his team was winning. So there are no odds anywhere in it, by design, and there never will be.

What you get instead

Live scores for every game, a live game tracker with a field view and a replay you can scrub through, play-by-play, box scores, team stats, standings, rankings and league leaders. Pin your teams and they sit at the top of the scores page, with alerts when they kick off, when it is close late, and the final.

Leagues covered

NFL, college football (FBS, FCS, Division II and Division III), NBA, men's and women's college basketball, MLB, NHL, WNBA and major soccer leagues.

Questions

Does Fumblerooski show betting odds or spreads?

No. There are no odds, spreads, over/unders, player props or sportsbook ads anywhere in the app.

Is Fumblerooski free?

Yes. It is free, with no account and no sign up.

How do I install it?

Open fumblerooski.app on your phone. On iPhone, tap Share then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap Install when your browser offers it. It then opens like any other app.
