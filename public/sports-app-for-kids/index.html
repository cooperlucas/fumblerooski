Fumblerooski

A sports app you can hand to your kid

Kids who love sports end up in sports apps, and sports apps are now full of betting. Fumblerooski has none of it.

Open Fumblerooski

Free. No account. Works on iPhone and Android: open it, then Add to Home Screen.

What it leaves out

No betting lines or odds. No sportsbook or casino ads, or ads of any kind. No chat, comments or messages, and no way to contact other users. No account, so nothing to sign up for and no personal information collected.

What it keeps in

The fun part: live scores, a live game tracker with a field view, player and team stats, standings and league leaders. Young fans can follow their favorite teams and players and actually learn the stats.

Made by a coach

Fumblerooski was built by a high school football coach who wanted a sports app he would be fine with his players using.

Questions

Is Fumblerooski safe for kids?

It was made to be. There is no betting content, no ads, no chat and no account, and it collects nothing about who uses it.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Open fumblerooski.app and add it to your home screen.
