Privacy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Fumblerooski doesn't collect anything about you. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no selling data. There's nothing to sell.

What stays on your phone

The app remembers a few things in your browser's storage on your own device, so it works the way you left it:

  • The teams you pin
  • Your settings: light or dark mode, which leagues show and in what order
  • Small conveniences, like the last stats tab you looked at and team colors it has already looked up

None of this leaves your phone, and nobody else can see it, including us. Clearing your browser's website data, or deleting the app from your home screen, erases it.

Who the app talks to

  • ESPN, for scores, schedules, standings and stats. Your phone asks ESPN for them directly, the same way any website loads, so ESPN sees an ordinary request from your device. Their own privacy policy covers that.
  • Google Fonts, for the typeface the app uses.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the app. Like any web host, it handles the requests that load the app's pages.

Visit counts

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages get used. It uses no cookies, doesn't fingerprint your device, and doesn't follow you around the web. It only tells us totals, like how many people opened the scores page today.

Kids

Fumblerooski is made to be safe for sports fans of any age: no betting lines, no ads, no chat, and no way to contact other users. We don't knowingly collect anything from anyone, kids included.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so first, with a new date at the top.
